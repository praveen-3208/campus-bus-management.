Android Setup Guide
  1. Install the free IP Webcam app by Pavel Khlebovich from Google Play Store.
  2. Connect your Android phone to the same Wi-Fi network as this laptop/PC.
  3. Open the app, scroll to the bottom, and tap Start Server.
  4. Look at the bottom of your phone screen for the URL, e.g. http://192.168.1.15:8080.
  5. Enter that URL into the settings box above and click Save & Connect.
  6. Position the phone camera facing the gate driveway.
